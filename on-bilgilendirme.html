3. The service

Makas Pro is a digital software subscription giving access to the features of the editing plugin for Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve that are available at the time, on up to 2 computers per account. Available today: silence and filler-word cuts, captions, syncing cameras and recorder audio by sound, one-click edit, audio clean-up and updates. Color, effects, the sound effects library, MultiCam and Shorts are in development, shown as “Coming soon” on the site, and are not part of the service at the time of purchase. Requires macOS or Windows with Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve Studio, which are not included.

4. Price and payment

The total including taxes for the period you choose (monthly or yearly) is shown at checkout; taxes are calculated by Paddle. Paid upfront; the subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Price changes are announced before renewal.

5. Delivery

Your account becomes Makas Pro immediately after payment. There is no physical delivery.

6. Withdrawal and refunds

Under Article 15(ğ) of the Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal does not apply to services performed immediately in electronic form. Nevertheless we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee after every payment, including renewals. See the Refund Policy.

7. Cancelling

Cancel any time from Manage card and invoices on your account page; you keep access until the end of the paid period.

8. Complaints and disputes

Email support@makasai.net. Consumers resident in Turkey may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committee within the annual monetary limits, or to Consumer Courts above them.

3. Hizmetin temel nitelikleri

  • Makas Pro: Adobe Premiere Pro ve DaVinci Resolve için kurgu eklentisinin kullanıma açık özelliklerine erişim sağlayan dijital yazılım aboneliği. Bugün açık olan özellikler: sessizlik ve dolgu sesi kesimi, altyazı, kamera ve ses kayıtlarını sese göre eşitleme, tek tıkla kurgu, ses temizleme ve güncellemeler. 4. Fiyat ve ödeme
