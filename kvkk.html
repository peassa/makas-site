  • Preventing trial abuse, account and system security, fraud prevention: our legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f).
  • Legal retention and reporting duties, including consent records under Law No. 6563: legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç).

Billing records are kept by Paddle.

You may ask whether your data is processed and for details, learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly, know the recipients in Turkey or abroad, ask for correction or deletion, ask us to notify recipients, object to solely automated decisions against you and claim compensation for unlawful processing.

Email support@makasai.net from the address on your account, or write to the address above. We respond free of charge within 30 days.
